These are module priorities, not rigid templates.

## 1. Photography and fashion

Prioritize:

- subject presence and body orientation
- clothing construction and fabric tension
- shot size and camera position
- light quality and skin rendering
- background simplicity
- filmic surface characteristics when desired

Useful language:

`editorial fashion photograph, restrained wide-angle perspective, soft diffused key light, natural skin texture, subtle grain, controlled highlights, quiet negative space`

Avoid automatic `85mm`, `f/1.4`, or camera-brand metadata unless requested or visually justified.

## 2. Cinematic stills

Prioritize:

- active story moment
- blocking and screen direction
- camera placement
- practical and motivated light sources
- atmospheric depth
- movement cues
- film texture and dynamic range

Avoid using `cinematic` as a substitute for actual staging.

## 3. Anime and illustration

Prioritize:

- character design language
- silhouette
- expression and pose
- line weight
- cel shading versus painterly rendering
- background treatment
- color script
- key-visual composition

For adult characters, say `adult` when age ambiguity could matter. Avoid generic tag piles unless converting for a tag-trained downstream model.

## 4. Product and advertising

Prioritize:

- exact product geometry
- material behavior
- brand-safe color system
- camera and surface relationship
- shadow/reflection control
- clean negative space for copy
- exclusions of unsupported extra products or props

Useful positive constraint:

`exactly one product is fully visible, centered on a clean surface, with an uninterrupted silhouette and no additional packaging or accessories.`

## 5. Poster and cover

Prioritize:

- title first when it is the core deliverable
- safe margins
- explicit line breaks
- subject-text separation
- hierarchy
- broad shapes rather than tiny decorative particles
- readable contrast

Example structure:

`A vertical 3:4 promotional cover. At the top center, two-line bold condensed sans-serif typography reads "KJ结构化提示" on the first line and "FAST单模型" on the second...`

## 6. Architecture and interior

Prioritize:

- geometry and spatial organization
- camera height and lens character
- material transitions
- openings and light direction
- human scale
- clutter control

Avoid impossible spatial relationships by explicitly stating floor, wall, ceiling, doorway, and furniture positions.

## 7. Chinese ink and mixed media

Prioritize:

- paper texture
- ink density and edge behavior
- negative space
- brush energy
- mineral pigment or cinnabar accents
- layering of traditional and contemporary elements

Avoid drowning the image in tiny particles. Use broad ink masses, controlled splashes, and calligraphic rhythm.

## 8. Surreal and abstract

Establish a physical or compositional logic even when the idea is impossible:

- what transforms into what
- scale relationships
- continuity of material
- gravity or flow direction
- background simplicity
- one dominant visual metaphor

This prevents “random surreal clutter.”

## 9. Controlled sensuality

For mature fashion-oriented sensuality:

- clearly establish the subject as an adult
- focus on confident pose, elegant silhouette, tailoring, lighting, and editorial presentation
- avoid age ambiguity so all subjects read unambiguously as adults
- use garment length, fit, neckline, body orientation, and camera height as precise controls
